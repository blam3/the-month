# Scientific evaluation status

This is a research-task prototype, not a validated psychological instrument.
The browser checks establish that choices and budget events can be recorded and
exported correctly. They do not establish measurement validity, reliability,
individual trait recovery, or causal effects of scarcity.

`recover.py` is a legacy exploratory parameter-recovery scaffold under a chosen
synthetic decision model. It is not a fitted model of participant behavior,
and its reported recovery under its own assumptions is not construct validation.
It has not been rerun as part of this packaging/export repair. The
[original notes](archive/original-recovery-notes.md) are retained for provenance;
stronger claims there should not be treated as established findings.

Before collecting research data: specify the measurement model and estimands;
check identifiability and parameter recovery under competing data-generating
models; separate effects of trial order, payoff, and budget; plan reliability
and external-validity studies; and obtain the appropriate research approvals.
A rising risk-choice rate alone does not identify a desperation threshold.
The posted task length is not established to support individual assessment.
